
Librery: Strategic & Tactical Domain-Driven Design
1. Strategic DDD: Bounded Contexts & Federation
Librery avoids the complexity of Multi-Tenancy database schemas. Every instance considers itself the Local authority.
Federation: External branches or university partners are treated as FederatedPeers. Inter-library interactions occur via standard Open Host Service (OHS) protocols over HTTP/3.
Core Context: Circulation (Event-Sourced Ledger).
Supporting Contexts: Cataloging (CRDTs), Patron Management, Financials.
Anti-Corruption Layer (ACL): External integrations (SIP2 Kiosks, Legacy database dumps) are mediated strictly by WASM Plugins to protect the internal models.
2. Tactical DDD: The Circulation Core
The Circulation Core is modeled as an Event-Sourced Aggregate.

Commands, Events & the Rules Engine
Commands (Intents) are injected with non-deterministic time by the Shell. Events (Facts) are appended to the ledger.

Authorization Context (Biscuit Tokens)
The Core is ignorant of cryptography. The Shell verifies the Biscuit Token (via an HttpOnly cookie or offline public key) and injects the deterministic capabilities (e.g., Capability::IssueLoans) into the CommandContext.
